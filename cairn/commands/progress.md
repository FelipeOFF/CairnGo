Summarize roadmap and phase completion from `.planning/` itself — the
`## Progress` table of ROADMAP.md plus STATE.md's position. **cairn does not
vendor `progress`**: v1.6 vendored the four cycle verbs and nothing else, and
this view needs no verb, only the two files. Report phases complete / total,
the active phase, and the next action. For the operational view that also folds
in beads ready/blocked work, use `/cairn:status`.
