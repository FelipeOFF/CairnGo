# Phase 12 Plan 01 — say what can run at once, and use the screen

## Why

The graph is read and the panel is built; nothing yet answers "can I run two of
these at the same time?", which is the question that decides whether a milestone
takes a day or three. And the board that was meant to carry all of this still
sat in a 1024px column with a third of a desktop empty beside it.

## Tasks

1. **`parallelism()`** beside the model: which pending phases nothing still
   open blocks, which are waiting, and a sentence describing the split in
   actual commands. Carry a `declared` flag so a roadmap with no recorded
   dependency is reported honestly rather than as universal independence.

2. **Render it** in the terminal panel (wrapped, not truncated — it is a
   sentence, and the truncated half is the qualifying one) and in the HTML,
   where the accent edge is spent only when there is more than one runnable
   phase.

3. **`/cairn:autonomous` announces its order**: resolve from
   `cairn-status --json` rather than re-reading ROADMAP.md, then state the
   order, the per-phase reason and the concurrency that is available but not
   being used.

4. **Fill the desktop**: grow the measure with the viewport, bounded, with
   prose keeping its own `ch` maximum.

5. **Tests and docs** for all of it.

  scroll, columns stacking below 760, prose at ~55 characters throughout.
